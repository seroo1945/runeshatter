01 / THE WORLD

AN OATH
SHATTERED.

Seven hundred years ago, Serath founded the Order of the Seven Wardens. Its pact with the Voice Beyond the Veil created seven seals: a prison built not only to contain a terrible power, but to hold innocent souls captive.

Desperate to bring back his daughter Elira, Aldren Veyr broke the Seal of Silence. Something answered in her voice, but it was the Voice Beyond the Veil. Your journey begins in the ruined village of Ravenholt, where signs of the Order point toward a secret buried for centuries.

02 / THE BATTLE

EVERY MATCH
MATTERS.

Every move is a decision. Match elemental runes on a 7×7 board, trigger explosive combinations, build energy for class abilities and survive enemies that fight back.

TACTICAL COMBAT

Match eight rune types to attack, shield and heal. Create explosive and universal runes with longer combinations.

GROW YOUR POWER

Choose Warrior, Hunter or Mage. Unlock abilities, upgrade runes and equip weapons, armor and rare artifacts.

EARN YOUR VICTORIES

Earn powerful equipment and uncover the secrets of Valdoria across 25 unique encounters. Progress through an expanding campaign filled with challenging battles, valuable discoveries and dark fantasy storytelling.
